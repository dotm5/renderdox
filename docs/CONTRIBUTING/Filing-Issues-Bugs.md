# Reporting a RenderDox observation

[Issues and ideas](Filing-Issues.md) · [Project showcase](../../README.md)

Describe what happened and what you expected. Useful context may include the build or
commit, Windows version, graphics API, target application, launch or injection route,
and the capture/event involved.

A screenshot, log excerpt, capture or short sequence of steps can help explain the observation.
Share whichever material is available and suitable to publish; an executable or RDC upload
is not a prerequisite for opening an issue.

Reports belong in the [RenderDox issue tracker](https://github.com/dotm5/renderdox/issues).
